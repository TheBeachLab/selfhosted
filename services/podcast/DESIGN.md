User correction after concept: keep the original Arroyo logo; remove Quellen
and promotional filler. The resulting spec intentionally removes the two
about columns and source section, replaces the concept's uppercase serif logo

Layout: white page, 1120px max-width, 87.8% container, two equal hero columns,
square unchanged cover left, regular Georgia title and short description right,
charcoal episode anchor, one upcoming-episode row, plain legal/footer links.
At <=700px use one column, full-width cover, stacked episode and wrapped footer.
Palette: #fff, #1d1d1f, #73737a, #e8e8ed. No green, no badges/cards.
Logo: exact live Arroyo header markup and font declarations read 2026-10-25;
Arial/Helvetica, lowercase arroyo, weight 500, tracking -1.2px, responsive
23–31px and subtitle separated by a thin vertical line. Heading weight 400.

Public copy: show title; mit Fran; brief show description; German / five-minute /
weekly planned format; Zur ersten Folge; episode title/description and recording
status. No published audio, fabricated episode dates or platform links.
Upcoming copy is explicitly public show metadata, never extracted from drafts.
